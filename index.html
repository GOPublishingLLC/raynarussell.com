Editor & Curator

Rayna Russell

An annual anthology of erotic short fiction — a new volume every year since 2022.

Rayna Russell curates. Each year she assembles a collection of explicit short fiction from a rotating cast of contributors, chosen for range rather than sameness: different narrators, different appetites, different rooms. The annual has run without a gap since 2022. Between volumes, she edits standalone collections built around a single premise.
